Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by Professor Junxian He. He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, with research interests in LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Education

- **Ph.D. in Computer Science**, Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), 2024–Present.
  - PhD candidate in the HKUST NLP Group; supervised by Professor Junxian He.
- **B.Eng.**, Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), 2020–2024 (graduated in June 2024).
  - Recipient of the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship, awarded by Shanghai Jiao Tong University.

## Research Experience
